Project Overview

Assumed direct accountability for evaluating line operations, cycle metrics, and ergonomics across active assembly lines at AboveAir Technologies. The primary goal was to eliminate motion waste, stabilize production scheduling, and identify key operational bottlenecks.

Engineering Methodology & Execution

  • Time & Motion Studies: Measured cycle times across assembly, brazing, and wiring sequences to establish baseline metrics and locate primary lead-time bottlenecks.
  • Kaizen & 5S Workstation Redesign: Fabricated customized workstation layouts and tool placements to minimize material handling motion, resulting in $11,300 in annual cost savings.
  • Standardized Quality Checklists: Developed and deployed standardized inspection protocols across 3 production lines, cutting manufacturing defect rates by 25%.
  • Production Tracking Tools: Designed key performance indicator (KPI) tools to log real-time metrics for cycle times, scrap, and operational costs.
